How to count
  • Count what's physically on the shelf right now — not what we've ordered.
  • Sections follow the walk order. Work through them top to bottom.
  • Enter whole containers in the box. For an opened container, pick ¼, ½, or ¾.
  • Count in the unit shown under each item (it matches how we buy it).
  • If there's none of something, tap None — that records 0 and shows it was checked. A blank means nobody checked.
  • Your count saves as a draft as you go. Anyone can pick it up on another device, and it's submitted once, when every item is counted.
