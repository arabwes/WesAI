Cafe MCP

Privacy notice

Effective September 3, 2026. Cafe MCP processes verified account identity, organization membership, permission settings, Toast connection metadata, encrypted Toast API credentials, and limited security audit records to provide the service.

Toast data

Read results are normally returned directly. Non-sensitive results larger than the inline limit may be encrypted and stored for up to 24 hours. PII, order, labor, and cash results are never persisted. Toast access tokens stay only in short-lived Durable Object memory.

Retention and sharing

Sanitized audit events are retained for 90 days and exclude request parameters and response bodies. Service providers may process data solely to operate authentication, hosting, storage, email, and support. Cafe MCP does not sell personal data.

Your choices

Owners can disconnect integrations, delete credentials and organizations, and revoke members. Users can revoke MCP grants. Request access or deletion through the support and deletion page.
