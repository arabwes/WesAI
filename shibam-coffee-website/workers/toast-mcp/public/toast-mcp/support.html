Support and data deletion

Use the portal to disconnect a Toast integration, permanently erase a stored credential, revoke a member, revoke an MCP grant, or delete an organization.

If you cannot sign in, email support@shibamatlanta.com from the verified address on your account. Include your organization name and the request type, but never include a Toast secret or access token. Identity verification may be required before account-level data is disclosed or deleted.

Security reports may be sent to security@shibamatlanta.com.
